## The entry

The word this entry would erase from the dictionary forever is:

> **defenestration**

Character count: **14**

Erase it, and the language loses its only word for throwing someone out of a
window — every future defenestration would have to be described, at length,
by people standing next to an open window with no word for what just happened.

## How it works

`main.py` holds the entry word as a constant, validates it (non-empty, exactly
one word, no whitespace, 1–77 characters), and writes it to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`).

```sh
python3 main.py
```
